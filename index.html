Make a plan for the money you have today.

When income is irregular, knowing what must be paid and how long your money needs to last can make the next decision clearer.

No bank connection. No M-Pesa login. No account required.

1. Money available

Enter money you can use now. Keep expected income separate.

Expected income is not counted as money available until you receive it.

2. What must be paid?

Add expenses due before your next income.

Only include expenses still unpaid. Avoid entering account numbers or other sensitive details.

Built around trust

  • Your entries are calculated in this browser and are not sent to a Nafasi server by this demo.
  • This demo does not save your plan. Refreshing the page may reset the numbers.
  • Use made-up figures when testing or showing the demo to others.
  • No financial outcome is guaranteed, and this is not personalised investment or lending advice.

Before you rely on your plan

  • Check that the listed expenses cover food, transport, bills, dependants and other needs.
  • Expected income can arrive late or be less than expected.
  • If essential costs exceed available money, a calculator cannot solve the shortfall. Consider contacting a trusted support organisation or creditor early where appropriate.
